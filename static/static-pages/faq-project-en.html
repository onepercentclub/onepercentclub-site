GENERAL

1. Why should I use 1%Club?

There are several reasons why 1%Club is the place to be.

  • 1%Club offers you tools to raise funds within your own network in an easy and fun way.
  • 1%Club offers you easy to use crowdsourcing tools.
  • You can learn from and work together with other people with smart ideas

2. How does it work?

Good question! Here is how it works. It all starts with a pitch. Once you have submitted your pitch, we'll check whether it meets our basis criteria. Yes? We'll post your pitch online! No? We'll send you an e-mail explaining why it did not meet our criteria. Once your pitch is online, you can write your plan. The plan is more detailed than the pitch and should give us more insights. Need help with your long term strategy or something else? Ask our crowd to help you by creating a 1%Skills task. Once you feel that you've provided us with all the info, you click on the submit button. We'll check if your plan meets our criteria. It does? You can start your campaign! If it doesn't meet our criteria we'll provide you with one chance to improve your plan. If your plan still doesn't meet our criteria, we'll reject it. Once you enter the campaign phase, you should be ready to rock. If you succeed in collecting your target amount, we will transfer the money to your account and you can start showing us the first results. The first design, the foundation, the first trainee etc. Once you have excuted your project you need to write a short evaluation and then it's time to.... celebrate!! whoop whoop! 

PITCH

1. How will you review my pitch?

1%Club checks if your pitch meets the following criteria:

  • Your idea creates a better world
  • It’s obvious how the money will be spent
  • Needs people, special know-how and skills

We also check whether:

  • Your smart idea is not aimed in a whole at proselytizing, that's a fancy word for trying to convert people to another religion or (political) opinion
  • Your smart idea is not aimed at personal gain
  • Your smart idea is not aimed at something illegal, weapons, drugs and so on.

Finally:

  • Your plan must be written in understandable English or Dutch and should make sense to us.
  • You have a personalized 1%Member profile with your name and photo. We love to see who you are!

2. How do I write the best pitch possible?

The best advice we can give you would be to let somebody else read it. If they understand your smart idea and want to contribute, it’s a good pitch! But here are a few more guidelines:

  1. Start strong! Write a powerful, engaging title that goes right to the heart of your idea and make sure your pitch addresses all the most important issues. 
  2. Tell a story. Don’t just answer the questions, but make your pitch into a story that is compelling, convincing and positive. 
  3. Think about the ‘why’. Make sure people understand why your smart idea is important. 
  4. Less is more. Don’t include things that get in the way of explaining your smart idea. Keep it short and simple. 

3.  I’ve sent in my pitch. Now what?

The project team will review your pitch to see if it meets our criteria within 3 business days. If it does, we’ll publish it online and you can start writing your plan. If it doesn’t, we’ll send you a message to say which criteria your pitch did not meet. 


4. My pitch has been declined. Can I try again?

Sure!

5. My pitch has been approved. I'm really happy, but what should I do now?

Step 2 is giving us the details. We really liked your pitch, but need to see that your plan really does meet our criteria. Need help? Ask our crowd by creating a 1%Skills task. 

CAMPAIGN PLAN

1. How do you review my plan?

First, we check if you have filled in all the details and uploaded a clear copy of your ID. Then we’ll check the following criteria:

  • Your idea makes a world of difference
  • It’s obvious how the money will be spent
  • You need between € 250 and € 5.000 to make it happen.
  • Needs people, special know-how and skills

We also check whether:

  • Your smart idea is not aimed in a whole at proselytizing, that's a fancy word for trying to convert people to another religion or (political) opinion
  • Your smart idea is not aimed at personal gain
  • Your smart idea is not aimed at something illegal, weapons, drugs and so on.

Finally:

  • Your plan must be written in understandable English or Dutch and should make sense to us.
  • You have a personalized 1%Member profile with your name and photo. We love to see who you are!

2. How do I make a good video pitch?

Remember that your video doesn’t need to be fancy, it just needs to be real and convincing! Here are some more guidelines:

  • Your video pitch shouldn’t be longer than two minutes. 
  • Tell the story (who, what, where, why and how) 
  • Use simple language 
  • Don’t use too much data, concentrate on personal stories instead 
  • Keep it simple 
  • Practice and review the video before you send it in! Make sure the basics – light and sound - are right so we can see and hear you! 

3. Why do I need to upload a copy of my passport ID?

Because we want to know if you’re real. Please make sure the copy of your passport/idea is valid and we can clearly see your picture on it. If your copy does not meet the criteria, we have to reject your campaign.

4. What Type of Bank Account Can I Use? 

You can use a business or personal account.

5. What’s IBAN?

For European accounts, you will be required to provide an IBAN (International Bank Account Number) The IBAN code is the same as your bank account number, but with a few extra numbers at the beginning. You can find it on your bank statements,

6. What’s a SWIFT code?

A SWIFT code is an 8- or 11-digit alphanumeric code that is used to identify a bank in an international wire transfer. If you're not sure of your SWIFT code, call your bank. Every bank has one!

TELLING YOUR STORY

1. I’m executing my project and some things didn’t go as planned. What should I do?

Don’t worry! It’s pretty normal. The only thing you need to do is be honest and tell us and your supporters what happened. People will appreciate your honesty and believe even more in your ideas!

2. I’m not really a good storyteller. What should I do?

No problem. You can’t be good at everything, right? Find somebody in your network to help you. Is there nobody in your network who can help you? Then find somebody in our network! Upload a 1%Skills task to ask for storytelling advice or feedback on your storytelling. 

3. Why do I have to write blogs in all the phases?

Crowdfunding and crowdsourcing is all about marketing. If you want to interest people in your project you need to tell a good story – and once they’re interested you need to keep them interested by sharing updates on your progress. It’s normal - remember successful brands like Adidas keep telling and updating their story all the time.

CROWDFUNDING CAMPAIGN

1. Is there a minimum or maximum amount that I can start a campaign for?

Yes, there is! Our minimum is € 250 and our max is  € 1.000.0000 Euro. However, we recommend asking for a small amount, as the less you ask for, the bigger the chances of you reaching your target!

2. How does crowdfunding work?

It‘s quite simple. Crowdfunding consists of two elements: the crowd and funding. When you crowdfund it means that you engage a large group of people (your crowd) to help you fundraise. It’s essential to keep everyone in your crowd on board by giving regular and honest updates.

3. How much service fee do you charge?

We charge a service fee of 7% if you reach your crowdfunding target. Were you not able to reach your target? Don’t worry, you can keep the amount your raised minus 12%. 

4. Why do you charge a service fee?

The service fee is partly used to cover our services and partly to cover the costs of bank transfers. We aim to cover our own costs with this service fee so we don’t need to be dependent on external funding.

5. How much time do I have to raise money? 

You have a maximum of 100 days! Go go go!

6. Is there a minimum amount raised before you will pay-out?

Yes, you have to crowdfund at least € 100. 

7. I’m new at crowdfunding. Could you give some tips& tricks?

Sure! Over the years we’ve learned the following:

  • Find 5-10 people to help promote your project. If they invite their network to join in, and those people invite their network, your reach instantly becomes much bigger! 
  • Decide on your strategy before you start. Who is your direct network and how much can they contribute in terms of money? Who has an interesting network and could become your promoter? If your direct network can donate the first 20%, people will be more likely to come and join it; people love to help a successful crowdfunding project. 
  • Take the time to contact people individually. It’s more effort, but it really makes a difference. And always remember to thank everyone personally for their support! 
  • Create urgency – keep people informed about the timeline and deadline. 
  • Keep the connection with your supporters active. Thank people for their donation or think of something to give back, like a beautiful picture of the project or a video message. You could also add incentives for more support: for €20 you get a personal picture, for €100 you receive a personal video, etc. 
  • Tell the story. Again, it all begins and ends with your story. Make sure your pitch is on edge and keep people informed. 
  • Keep your project page alive. Refresh it with regular updates, share photos and videos on a frequent basis and spread these within the networks that you have mobilized.

Need more tips & tricks? Click here!

8. I’m not that good at social networks, but I do have a big network.

Crowdfunding is about the crowd. Social networks help, but if you have a large network in “the real word” that also helps. Of course you can always ask your network to spread it through social media.

9. I really do not like to bother my own network. What should I do?

Hmmm.. that might be a problem. Remember that you do not necessarily need to ask your network for money, but can also ask them to help you make this campaign a success by spreading it in their network. But try to strategize this question as much as possible before you start your crowdfunding campaign. 

10. I’ve waiting for funds to come in, but I’ve only raised a small percentage so far.

Sometimes people are lucky and support for their campaign comes in immediately from all corners of the world. However, for the other 99% it starts with their own network and their networks’ network. This basically means you have to tell people about your project on 1%Club. Once, twice, maybe even three times or more! Talk to people with email, Facebook, Twitter, face-to-face, by phone, with a poster or in a video message; whatever you need to do to get them on board! You’ll have to put in a lot work to reach your target - but remember, hard work tends to pays off!

11. How do I reach out to the 1%Club community?

You gather most attention from our community by writing frequent updates, posting pictures and videos. They love to see what happens and hear your story! However, don’t count on their support, but think of a strategy to make it anyway. 

12. Can I edit my plan during my crowdfunding campaign?

Once you have submitted your plan to the 1%Club, you are no longer able to edit your plan. So make sure your state everything clearly and perfectly, because you only get one shot.

13. How can my campaign become more visible on the website?

The more donations you receive in a short period of time, the faster your project gains popularity. The most popular projects appear on top of the page. It is thus directly linked to the success of your crowdfunding campaign.

14. What should I do if I reach my target?

Go to you campaign page and write a short thank you note! They love to hear your first thoughts!

1%SKILLS TASKS

1. I want to upload a task. What should I do to make sure I find the right person?

  • Keep things simple. Breaking a problem into smaller, simpler tasks works much better than one big task. 
  • Make sure your task is SMART: Significant, Meaningful, Attainable, Relevant and Timely 
  • Choose the right crowd; choose that special someone or someones that are the perfect match for your task. 
  • Remember that crowdsourcing takes time. People usually do it in their spare time, so make sure you keep people up to date with progress and respond as quickly as possible to any questions and remarks. 
  • Provide some direction. Offer help along the way and step in to keep things focused when minds start to drift. 
  • Don’t forget to let us know when it is time to celebrate the results!

2. Four people responded to my task, but I only need one. What should I do?

Wow, you’ve uploaded a very popular task! There are two things you can do:

  1. Simply pick the one you think is the most suitable based on what they’ve written. 
  2. Ask them all to do a part of the task, take a good look at what they’ve delivered and choose the one that you like best. Good luck! And don’t forget to send a message to the people that you choose not to work with. 

3. I found a skilled person to execute my 1%Task. What should I do now?

Just click on the button "change status" next to task and change his or her status. The person that applied will receive a notification that you assigned her/him to the task. 

4. My task is no longer relevant. What should I do?

We hope this is good news! You can easily close the task via your dashboard. Just click on "edit task".  

PAYMENTS

1. Do I need to save receipts for anything I spend?

It’s not strictly necessary, but if you really want to be transparent you can make a picture of any of your receipts and upload them to your project page.

2. What if I collected funds for my campaign?, but didn’t manage to collect the entire budget?

Don’t worry! You’ll receive the money you raised minus 12% service fee. 

3. What’s happens after I funded my campaign?

  • We’ll transfer the money within two months and will send you a notification of this if you have a non-Dutch bank account.
  • We advice you to keep telling your story to the crowd while executing your project.  Tell us about the first steps, the first stone, the first training session. Upload pictures and videos to tell you story.
  • Finished the implementation? Celebrate your lessons learned with us by answering a couple of questions.

4. One of my supporters wants the correct documents for tax deduction after his/her gift. What can I tell you?

Every 1%Member can access their donation overview through MY1% - My Donations. This is all they need!

EVALUATION

1. Why should I write an evaluation?

Because your lessons learned might help other change makers! And because we love to celebrate the results!

2. Evaluation? That sounds like a lot of work and the boring part.

No, it shouldn't be! We suggest to keep it short and sweet. You know what they say: images speak louder than words. So rather than 10 pages of text, write a short blog and upload lots of pictures or even a video! Please answer the following questions in your blog:

WHAT AND HOW: What were your targets and did you reach them? How many people directly benefited and if different from the original number please explain why

CHANGE:  What feedback did you receive from your targetgroup? We would love to hear their story!

TIPS AND TRICKS: What are the tips & tricks you would give to other campaigners?

FINANCIAL: how is the money spent? Were there alterations in the budget? And if so, which one(s) and why?

WHAT’S NEXT: what is the follow-up of the project?
Be honest. Your story is all that counts!
3. What should I do after I write an evaluation?

Celebrate! Of course, if you want, you can upload a new pitch to 1%Club. You’ve already succeeded once, so why not twice?
