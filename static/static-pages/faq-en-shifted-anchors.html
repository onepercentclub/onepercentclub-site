1. WHAT IS 1%Club?
1%Club is the go-to crowdfunding platform for doing good. We empower people with great ideas to create positive change in their community, city, country – or the whole world – by enabling them to raise funds and kick-start initiatives with our innovative tools. We aim to make crowdfunding the most simple and rewarding experience for everyone in the world who wants to do good.

 

2. WHAT IS 1%Club’s DREAM?
1%Club aims to be the go-to crowdfunding platform for doing good. We want to do this by making it as simple and rewarding as possible for everyone who wants to make the world a better place.

 

3. WHY 1%?
We want to make crowdfunding the most simple and rewarding experience for everyone who wants to do good in the world. That's why we are the go-to crowdfunding platform to kick-start initiatives that really can make a difference, one small change at a time.

 

4. WHAT IS SO UNIQUE ABOUT 1%Club?

  • The go-to crowdfunding platform for doing good.

We empower people with great ideas to create positive change in their community, city, country – or the whole world over.

  • Fundraising tools to activate your network.

We enable you to raise funds and kick-start your initiative via your own network. We take care of administration and your own page to help you concentrate on reaching your target.

  • We’re here to help
 

Friends help each other – and we’ll make crowdfunding the most simple and rewarding experience possible for you. If you need us, we’re always just one phone call away.

5. WHAT HAS 1%Club ACHIEVED UP UNTIL NOW?
Since we started in 2008 together we have crowdfunded more then 1 million euros over 525 campaigns in more than 70 countries all over the world.

1. WHO CAN JOIN 1%Club?
You, me and everyone you know! Individuals, associations, schools, foundations and businesses…everyone is welcome to start or join a campaign and change the world!

 
 

2. WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF JOINING 1%Club?
You know your money, skills and time go directly where they're needed most.
You contribute to campaigns that have a clear goal, are result-oriented, are small-scale and therefore easy to realise.
You can follow the progress of the campaigns you support.
You can choose to take as big or small a role in a campaign as you wish.

 

3. HOW DO I GET STARTED?
Easy! Find a campaign you like and support it. Or start your own campaign here.

 

4. CAN I SUPPORT CAMPAIGNS MORE STRUCTURALLY ON 1%Club?
Yes, just become our friend! Our 1%FRIENDS all share a little every month. All their 1%s combined realise more projects and change more lives for the better. Doesn’t that sound like a truly meaningful friendship?

How? There are two ways:

1. Donate to a campaign every month until it’s finished. When it reaches its target, you can choose a new campaign to support.

2. Donate every month to the top 3 most popular campaigns. The campaigns that are most successful in finding supporters get rewarded with your donation.

To become a 1%FRIEND, just click here.

 

5. HOW DO YOU PROTECT MY PRIVACY?
The privacy and data security of our members is very important to 1%Club. We take great care to protect your personal data against any form of hacking or illegal processing. Passwords, for example, are encoded, so that even the 1%Crew cannot access them. You can read more about 1%Clubs privacy policy here.

 

6. HOW DO I DELETE MY PROFILE?
Let’s start by saying that we hate saying goodbye. Thank you for your support and you’re welcome back anytime! You can find a link to delete your profile on your profile page.

1. WHAT IS CROWDFUNDING? 
Crowdfunding is the collective effort of a large group of people who financially - with relatively modest contributions - support initiatives by other people or organisations. Funding targets are reached by promoting the campaign through communities, social media and local networks. Mobilising (social) networks is crucial. Crowdfunding isn’t easy, but always rewarding!

 

2. HOW CAN I PLEDGE MONEY TO SUPPORT A CAMPAIGN?
First choose a project that you like! Click on the support button, decide on the amount of money you want to contribute, if it is a one-off donation or monthly and how you want to pay. That’s it!

 

3. WHAT HAPPENS WITH MY DONATION TO A CAMPAIGN THAT DOES NOT REACH ITS TARGET?
That's bad news! We hate it when that happens. However, the good news is that even if a campaign doesn’t reach its target, they will still receive the money they raised.

 

4. HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE FOR DONATIONS TO APPEAR ON THE SITE?
One-off donations are immediately visible on the campaign page. Monthly donations become visible on the corresponding campaign page(s) at beginning of every new month.

 

5. WHAT ARE YOUR FEES? HOW MUCH GOES TO THE CAMPAIGN?
1%Club applies a 7% fee to the funds collected if the campaign meets its target. If the target is not reached, we apply a fee of 12%. This is to cover processing fees and transaction costs.

 

6. CAN I MAKE A DONATION ANONYMOUSLY?
Yes, you can!

 

7. ARE DONATIONS TO 1%Club TAX DEDUCTIBLE?
1%Club is a non-profit foundation with ANBI status (Public Benefit Organisation) under the name '1procentclub'. This means that donations to campaigns on 1%Club can be tax deductible. You can find more information on this on the Dutch Tax Revenue website.

 

8. DOES 1%Club HAVE A WARRANT FOR DUTCH CHARITABLE INSTITUTIONS?
1%Club has ANBI Status. We don’t have a CBF stamp because as a crowdfunding platform we don’t fit their traditional charity rules. We still try to follow their transparency and efficiency guidelines as much as possible.

 

9. HOW DO I SHARE 1% OF MY TIME AND SKILLS? (CROWDSOURCING)?
No one is good at everything (ok, except Chuck Norris). This includes the Campaigners at 1%Club. Sometimes their projects need some accountancy know-how, a fresh new logo or a marketing strategy in order to take off. Browse through the campaigns and see if they have tasks requiring people’s skills or expertise. Once you see a task you can support, click on ‘Apply for task’!

1. WHAT KIND OF CAMPAIGNS CAN I FIND ON 1%Club?
You can find all kind of campaigns on 1%Club, but they all have one thing in common: they all intend to make the world a better place.

 

2. WHY CROWDFUND VIA 1%Club?
We’re the go-to crowdfunding platform for doing good.
We empower people with great ideas to create positive change in their community, city, country, or the entire world!
We provide fundraising tools to activate your network.
We enable you to raise funds and kick-start your initiative via your own network. We take care of administration and make sure you can share your campaign page with your network.
We’re always here for you.
We’ll make crowdfunding the most simple and rewarding experience possible for you. If you need our help at any time, we’re always just one phone call away.

 

3. HOW DO I KNOW IF MY CAMPAIGN FITS 1%Club?
Simple! Go to Start Campaign and read the preconditions. We review every campaign before it goes online.

 

4. HOW DOES 1%Club SCREEN THE 1%CAMPAIGNS?
Our team checks every campaign to see if it meets our strict criteria before we put it online. Both the 1%Crowd and campaigners actively participate in the improvement and evaluation of their idea. They do this by following the agreed process, engaging in regular feedback sessions, and by participating through sharing expertise and knowledge.

 

5. HOW MANY CAMPAIGNS CAN BE LISTED ON 1%Club?
To give every campaign a high chance of success we ensure that the number of open campaigns remains in proportion with the number of participating 1%Members.

 

6. IS THERE A REASON BEHIND THE ORDER OF THE PRESENTATION OF CAMPAIGNS ON THE SITE? AND WHAT DETERMINES A CAMPAIGN’S POPULARITY?
The most popular campaigns appear at the top of the page. They are ranked by the number of donations as well as the subtotal of funds received. We chose donations as the determining factor because a contribution in money is easy to measure. Money talks! Time and expertise are harder to measure and can only be evaluated by estimates.

 

7. WHAT IF IT TURNS OUT THAT A CAMPAIGN HASN'T BEEN PROPERLY IMPLEMENTED OR FULFILLED?
Things don’t always go as expected. Everyone’s only human, right? As long as the campaigner keeps us and you updated about what is happening, we think it’s okay if they make some small mistakes on the way. However, if we see that a project has deliberately not been implemented properly, we will remove it from 1%Club, plus any funds received will be claimed back by 1%Club under the terms of the agreement that every Campaigner signs. The parties involved will not be allowed to submit a new project to 1%Club again.

1. WHO IS 1%Club?
We’re a Dutch non-profit foundation based in Amsterdam. 1%Club was founded in 2008 by Anna Chojnacka and Bart Lacroix. 1%Club’s mission is to enable people who want to do good in the world to kick-start a crowdfunding campaign and mobilise their network in the most simple and rewarding way.

 

2. WHO WORKS AT 1%Club?
At the moment there are fourteen passionate people working in AmLab in Amsterdam. We work hard to develop new ways to make our platform better, faster, easier and smarter. Our online team is always on the move, creating new ideas for better crowdfunding.
